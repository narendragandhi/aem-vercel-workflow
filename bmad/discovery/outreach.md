## What to Say When Interviewing

### Intro (30 sec)
"I'm building AEMFlow - a visual editor for AEM workflows. Quick 10 min to validate some assumptions. No selling - just learning."

### Key Questions
1. "How do you create AEM workflows today?" 
   - Listen for: XML, manual, other tools

2. "Roughly how many hours/week on workflow XML?"
   - Target: 5+ hours = validated pain

3. "Hardest part of AEM workflows?"
   - Listen for: debugging, trial/error, no visual

4. "If there was a visual drag-drop editor... would you use it?"
   - Target: Enthusiastic yes

5. "Would you pay $29/month?"
   - Listen for: yes, maybe, no

### Closing
"Can I follow up with you? And do you know other AEM devs who might want to try the beta?"
