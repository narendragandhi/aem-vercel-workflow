# AEMFlow User Discovery - MVP-000

## Objective

Validate product assumptions by interviewing AEM developers.

## Research Questions

| # | Question | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | How do you currently create AEM workflows? | Validate problem exists |
| 2 | How much time per week on XML workflow editing? | Quantify pain (target: 40%+) |
| 3 | What's the hardest part of AEM workflows? | Find #1 pain point |
| 4 | Would you pay for a visual workflow builder? | Validate willingness to pay |
| 5 | What would you pay? ($19/$29/$49) | Test price point |
| 6 | Would you try a free beta? | Validate acquisition |

## Interview Script

### Introduction (2 min)
"Hi, I'm building AEMFlow - a visual AI-powered workflow builder. Quick 10 min interview to validate some assumptions."

### Questions

1. **Current Workflow Creation**
   - "How do you typically create AEM workflows?"
   - "Do you use XML directly or a tool?"

2. **Time Spent**
   - "Roughly how many hours/week do you spend editing AEM workflow XML?"
   - "What's the most time-consuming part?"

3. **Pain Points**
   - "What's the hardest thing about AEM workflows?"
   - "Have you ever made a mistake that took hours to debug?"

4. **Solution Interest**
   - "If there was a visual drag-drop editor for AEM workflows, would you use it?"
   - "What features would you want?"

5. **Pricing**
   - "Would you pay for such a tool?"
   - "Would $29/month be too much, just right, or too cheap?"

6. **Beta Interest**
   - "Would you try a free beta?"
   - "Can I follow up with you?"
